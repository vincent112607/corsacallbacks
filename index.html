Dialpad & settings

Dialpad → Settings → My Company (or your profile) → Authentication → API Keys → Add a Key. The key is saved only in this browser on this computer. Keep the Dialpad desktop or web app open and signed in. Dialpad allows 5 dials per minute.
Email Dialpad support to get a mini dialer (CTI) Client ID. Give them http://localhost:8765 as the allowed origin. Then Dialpad loads inside this page, and Call dials from it.
